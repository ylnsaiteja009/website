About

Sweet Shell is a premium dessert destination created for people who love traditional Indian sweets and modern desserts. Our menu brings together classic Indian mithai, premium dry-fruit sweets, rich chocolate desserts, creamy ice creams, cakes and thick shakes. From a simple Gulab Jamun to an extravagant Ferrero-style dessert, there is something for every sweet lover.
